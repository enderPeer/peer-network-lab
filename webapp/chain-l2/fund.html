Fund the epochs

The claim contract pays only what the steward deposits behind a published root, and each deposit is two signatures: an approve, then openEpoch. This page reads every closed epoch off the host, says plainly which are worth funding and why the rest are not, and signs nothing itself — MetaMask asks you, once per transaction, each named before it arrives.

The epochs, as this host computes them
Ticked epochs are funded when you press the button below. An epoch is once per number, forever: its root and its deposit can never be changed, topped up or taken back early — only what nobody claims returns, to the steward, after the deadline.
